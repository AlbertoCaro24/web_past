CASA DE LA MONTA – IMIDRA, ARANJUEZ

TECHNICAL FIELD VISIT

  • 1st STOP: Cultural stop around the Royal Palace of Aranjuez: 1 hour.
  • 2nd STOP (12:00h): Casa de la Monta.
Exhibitions and Tours:
  • Photographic Exhibition “History of La Casa de la Monta” and “The horse in Spain”.
  • Monographic exhibition of native horse and donkey breeds.
  • Photographic Exhibition “Spanish Native Breeds” Bestiarium, by José Barea.
  • Main hall: 56 native breeds of sheep, goats, cows, and sheepdogs.
  • Wool textile art exhibition and traditional spinning initiation workshop.
  • Educational tour of the wool process.
Activities and Demonstrations:
  • Screening of the documentary “Transhumant shepherds, carding memories”.
  • Recital by Luis García Valera – Entresierras School of Popular Music.
  • Demonstration of traditional grazing and use of herding dogs.
  • Traditional shearing exhibition.
  • Pastoral food: Rice tasting and “Paquitos” (with INTEROVIC).
  • Tasting of products with the “100% Native Breed” logo (MAPA): cheeses and jerky from native breeds.
